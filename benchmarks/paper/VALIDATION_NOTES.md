## 4. Benchmark results (Tables 1-5) validation

Tables 1a-1c, 3, 4, and 5 were produced by running analysis scripts directly
against the benchmark output files on HTCF at
`/scratch/sahlab/leran/paper_benchmark/gut_10x/`:

- **Table 1a (QC):** Generated by `run_10x_benchmark.sh` and `rerun_realworld_v3.sh`,
  scored with `viroforge benchmark qc`
- **Table 1b (Assembly):** MEGAHIT and metaSPAdes results scored with
  `viroforge benchmark assembly`
- **Table 1c (Taxonomy):** Kraken2 and Kaiju results scored with
  `viroforge benchmark taxonomy`
- **Table 3 (QC FP/FN):** Generated by `analyze_qc_fp_fn.py` tracing every false
  positive and false negative to its per-read source label
- **Table 4 (Assembly ground truth):** Generated by `analyze_assembly_ground_truth.py`
  classifying chimeras and missing genomes by domain, family, size, and GC content
- **Table 5 (Taxonomy by source):** Generated by `analyze_taxonomy_by_source.py`
  joining classifier output with per-read source labels
